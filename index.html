Ion pairing bench

Drag each cation's marker toward the anion it should pair with. Position is charge-equivalent, so the mix always balances. Ca, K, NH₄, and Na get extra-cornered plots so citrate, MES, and the two phosphate species sit as real corners rather than a bolt-on slider. Lock a corner to hold its share fixed while you adjust the rest.

Experiments

Set the current mix as a baseline, then change something — a magnitude slider, a corner, whatever — and capture the result as an experiment. The summary explains what actually happened to each ion: was charge added, dispersed across several anions, or displaced away from one (usually a cap or a lock) onto others.
